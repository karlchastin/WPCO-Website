PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 2026

World Peace Control Organization ("WPCO", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of our community. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and safeguarding of information when you interact with our Verification System and associated Discord services.

1. Information Collection

To provide a secure and integrated experience, we may collect and process the following categories of information when you initiate the verification process:

  • Account Identifiers: Basic, publicly available identifiers from associated third-party platforms (such as your Discord User ID and Roblox User ID) to facilitate account linking.
  • Network & Telemetry Data: Standard connection data provided by your browser or network. This may include, but is not limited to, general network identifiers, browser metadata, and connection diagnostics. This data is collected automatically to ensure platform security.

2. Use of Information

We process the collected information solely for legitimate administrative and security purposes, including:

  • Authenticating your identity to grant access to restricted sections of the WPCO community.
  • Enforcing community guidelines by identifying, mitigating, and preventing ban evasion, abusive behavior, or unauthorized automated access.
  • Maintaining the structural integrity of our internal ranking and moderation systems.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

WPCO does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • When strictly necessary to comply with applicable laws, legal requests, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of WPCO and its users.
  • To our trusted administrative and moderation teams strictly for the purpose of community safety and enforcement.

4. Data Retention

Information collected during the verification process is retained within our secure, self-hosted databases for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. If you terminate your participation in the WPCO community, certain moderation or security-related records may be retained indefinitely to prevent future abuse.

5. Security Measures

We employ reasonable, industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. All data transmitted during the verification process is encrypted in transit using secure protocols (HTTPS).

6. Third-Party Services

Our Verification System interfaces with third-party APIs (e.g., Roblox OAuth2). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies before authorizing any connections.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the WPCO administrative team directly through our official Discord server.
