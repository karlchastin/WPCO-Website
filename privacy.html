Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

World Peace Control Organization ("WPCO", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our verification services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the WPCO Verification System, we collect the following information:

  • Discord User ID: To link your Roblox account to your Discord account.
  • Roblox User ID: Obtained via the official Roblox OAuth2 API to verify your identity.
  • Device Fingerprint & IP Address: Used strictly for anti-cheat and alternative account (alt) detection to maintain the integrity of our community.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To grant you the appropriate roles and access within the WPCO Discord Server.
  • To detect and link alternative accounts to ensure fair play and accurate ranking.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our community.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is kept strictly confidential and is only accessible by WPCO system administrators for moderation purposes.

4. Data Retention

Your verification data is retained in our secure database for as long as you remain a member of the WPCO community. If you leave the community, your data may be retained for moderation and security records.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All communication between your browser, Roblox, and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the WPCO administrative team via our official Discord server.
